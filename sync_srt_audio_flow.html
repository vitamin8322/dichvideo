Sync SRT + Audio Segments

Minh hoa cach tool cat theo SRT, so do dai audio, retime video/audio, render timeline va ghep thanh video cuoi.

Video goc Audio segments FFmpeg render

1. Input

Video goc Video 5 phut duoc doc duration, sau do tung moc SRT tro thanh mot doan can retime.
File SRT Moi block co start, end, text. Neu co 200 block thi co 200 segment chinh.
001 | 00:00:01,200 --> 00:00:03,600
002 | 00:00:04,100 --> 00:00:06,900
003 | 00:00:07,600 --> 00:00:09,200
Audio segments Tool sap xep theo so trong ten file: 0001.wav, 0002.wav, 0003.wav...

2. Tinh sync

So duration tung segment Tool tinh video_ratio va audio_tempo de dua video piece va audio piece ve cung target_duration.
Cong thuc chinh
video_duration = srt_end - srt_start
desired_ratio = audio_duration / video_duration
video_ratio = clamp(desired_ratio, min, max)
audio_tempo = audio_duration / target_duration
Ban update moi Video khong con encode tung file segment rieng. Tat ca video segment/gap/tail duoc gom vao mot filter_complex_script.
1 lan render video timeline

3. Output

retimed_video.mp4 Video da duoc cat, keo/cham/nhanh theo tung block SRT va concat trong FFmpeg filter.
retimed_audio.wav Audio segments duoc atempo, pad/trim, sau do concat thanh mot track audio lien mach.
final.mp4 Tool mux video da render voi audio da sync. Neu bat audio goc, audio goc duoc retime va mix truoc khi mux.
